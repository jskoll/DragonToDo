# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First public release of dragon-todo.

- Interactive todo.txt TUI with projects, contexts, subtasks, filtering,
  sorting, priorities, and due dates.
- Multi-line task descriptions with clickable detail URLs on macOS.
- Subtasks inherit their parent's projects, contexts, and due date.
- Completed tasks are hidden by default; press `H` to show all tasks.
- macOS launchd-based due-date notifications.
